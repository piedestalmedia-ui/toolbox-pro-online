What the QR Code Generator does

A QR code stores text that a compatible camera or scanner can read. Keep the payload concise and test the generated image with a phone before sharing or printing it.

Useful for creating a scannable link for a handout, poster, or presentation after checking the destination.

How to use this tool

  1. Step 1. Enter a URL or short text.
  2. Step 2. Choose Create QR code.
  3. Step 3. Scan the image with a phone to confirm it opens the intended content.

Limits and privacy

QR generation depends on the external provider being available. Do not submit private information, access tokens, or confidential URLs.

Unlike the other local tools, this tool sends the text you enter to the QR service at api.qrserver.com. Review that provider’s terms before use.

Frequently asked questions

Is QR generation fully private?

No. The text is sent to an external QR image service to create the image. Avoid entering confidential information.

Should I test the QR code before printing?

Yes. Scan it with a phone and verify the destination, especially before distributing printed copies.
