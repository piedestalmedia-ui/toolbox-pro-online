What the YouTube Hashtag Generator does

Hashtags can provide context and help viewers navigate related topics, but adding many unrelated tags can be confusing. This browser tool formats your own words; it does not research trending hashtags or measure reach.

Useful for quickly formatting a topic phrase as readable hashtag drafts for a video description or social post.

How to use this tool

  1. Step 1. Enter a concise topic.
  2. Step 2. Generate a short set of hashtag drafts.
  3. Step 3. Remove duplicates and unrelated terms before copying the result.

Limits and privacy

The suggestions are based on your input and simple phrase variations. They are not trend data or a promise of extra reach.

Hashtag suggestions are created locally from the text you type.

Frequently asked questions

Does this find trending hashtags?

No. It formats your topic and creates simple variations; it is not connected to live social trends.

How many hashtags should I use?

Choose a small, relevant set and follow the current rules of the platform where you plan to post.
