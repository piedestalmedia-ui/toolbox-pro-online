What the YouTube Description Extractor does

Video descriptions are displayed on YouTube and may change over time. This helper validates a link and gives you a direct route to the video, rather than scraping or storing its metadata.

Useful when you have a video link and want a quick way to open its description on the source platform.

How to use this tool

  1. Step 1. Paste a YouTube watch link, youtu.be link, or video ID.
  2. Step 2. Choose Open video details.
  3. Step 3. On YouTube, expand the description and copy the text you need.

Limits and privacy

This page does not extract or download descriptions. Login, age, region, or privacy restrictions on YouTube still apply.

The link is parsed in your browser. Opening the video contacts YouTube directly.

Frequently asked questions

Can it extract descriptions automatically?

No. YouTube does not expose descriptions to this static browser page, so it opens the video for you to view.

Can I use it with private videos?

The video page follows YouTube’s access controls; you need permission to view a private video.
