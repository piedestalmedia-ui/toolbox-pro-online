What the Image Compressor does

Smaller images can be easier to attach, share, or use on a web page. Compression depends on image dimensions, format, and content. JPEG quality controls are most effective for photographs; PNG output is lossless in this browser workflow and may not shrink much.

Useful for making a lighter copy of a photo before attaching it to a message or adding it to a page.

How to use this tool

  1. Step 1. Choose a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image.
  2. Step 2. Adjust the quality slider if it is available for your image format.
  3. Step 3. Review the new size and preview, then download the processed copy.

Limits and privacy

The tool produces one processed image at a time. Browser canvas may not preserve animation, metadata, or every format feature. A lower quality setting can visibly reduce detail.

Image pixels are processed on your device using the browser canvas. The file is not uploaded to ToolBox Pro.

Frequently asked questions

Will compression always make the file smaller?

Not always. The result depends on the source image and selected format. PNG images may remain similar in size, especially when they contain photographic detail.

Does the tool overwrite my original?

No. It creates a separate download and leaves your selected original file unchanged.
