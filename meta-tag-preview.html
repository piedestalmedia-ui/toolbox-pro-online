What the Meta Tag Preview does

Search engines may generate snippets from page content and can choose a different excerpt for different queries. This preview is an editorial aid; it cannot predict the exact title or description Google will show.

Useful when drafting metadata for a landing page or checking that a page summary is clear before publishing.

How to use this tool

  1. Step 1. Enter the page title, meta description, and URL.
  2. Step 2. Review the live preview as you edit each field.
  3. Step 3. Make the title and description specific to the page and accurate to its content.

Limits and privacy

The preview is not a Google Search simulation and does not verify your page, structured data, rankings, or indexing status.

Text and URLs are previewed in your browser and are not sent to a search engine.

Frequently asked questions

Will Google show this exact description?

Not necessarily. Search engines may choose a snippet from the page content that better matches a query.

Is there a fixed ideal character count?

There is no guaranteed fixed display length. Keep the summary concise and put the page’s main value first.
