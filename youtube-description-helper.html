What the YouTube Description Helper does

Descriptions help viewers understand what a video covers and where to find related resources. This helper produces a short starter paragraph and call to action; it does not know your video’s actual content, products, or claims.

A quick starting point for creators who want a consistent description outline before publishing.

How to use this tool

  1. Step 1. Enter the video topic and channel name.
  2. Step 2. Generate a draft description.
  3. Step 3. Edit it to match what appears in the video and add any useful links or disclosures.

Limits and privacy

The generated draft is generic and may omit important context. Check all claims and add required sponsorship or affiliate disclosures yourself.

The text you enter is handled in the page and is not sent to an AI service.

Frequently asked questions

Does it write a full transcript or AI summary?

No. It makes a short template from your topic; it does not watch or analyze your video.

Should I publish the draft as-is?

Review and edit it first so it accurately describes your video and includes the right links and disclosures.
