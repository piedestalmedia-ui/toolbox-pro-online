What the Case Converter does

The converter applies common case transformations in your browser. Title case here capitalizes the first letter of each word; it does not follow every publication’s house style or language-specific capitalization rules.

Useful for consistent headings, quick cleanup of text copied from another document, or changing label capitalization.

How to use this tool

  1. Step 1. Paste or type text into the editor.
  2. Step 2. Choose uppercase, lowercase, title case, or sentence case.
  3. Step 3. Review names and acronyms, then copy the converted result.

Limits and privacy

Automatic capitalization can alter proper nouns, abbreviations, and languages with special casing rules. Always review the result.

Frequently asked questions

What does title case mean here?

The tool capitalizes the first letter of each word. This is a simple rule, not a complete editorial style guide.

Can I undo a conversion?

Your input remains in the text box. Choose another case option or use your browser’s undo while editing.
