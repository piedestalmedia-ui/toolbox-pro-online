What the Image Aspect Ratio does

The calculator divides both dimensions by their greatest common divisor to produce a whole-number ratio. The decimal value is width divided by height, which can be compared with dimensions for another canvas.

Useful for checking proportions before creating a thumbnail, presentation image, or resized asset.

How to use this tool

  1. Step 1. Enter the image width in pixels.
  2. Step 2. Enter the image height in pixels.
  3. Step 3. Compare the simplified ratio or decimal value with your target dimensions.

Limits and privacy

An aspect ratio describes shape, not image quality or resolution. Different pixel dimensions can have the same ratio.

Dimensions are processed locally in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

What does 16:9 mean?

For every 16 units of width, there are 9 units of height. A 1920 by 1080 image has this ratio.

Will the tool resize the image?

No. It only calculates the ratio. Use the Image Resizer page to create a new image file.
