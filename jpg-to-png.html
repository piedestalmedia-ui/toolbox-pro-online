What the JPG to PNG does

JPEG and PNG use different image encoding. This utility decodes the selected JPEG in your browser canvas and exports the pixels as PNG. Converting a JPEG to PNG does not restore image detail lost when the JPEG was created.

Useful when an application or workflow specifically accepts PNG images, or when you want a common lossless output container for the decoded pixels.

How to use this tool

  1. Step 1. Select a JPG or JPEG file.
  2. Step 2. Choose Convert image.
  3. Step 3. Preview the output and download the PNG copy.

Limits and privacy

A PNG version of a JPEG is not automatically smaller or sharper. JPEG source artifacts remain in the converted image; the conversion cannot recreate missing detail.

Conversion takes place in the browser on your device. No image upload is made to ToolBox Pro.

Frequently asked questions

Does PNG conversion improve image quality?

No. The PNG file keeps the decoded pixels, but it cannot restore details already lost in the original JPEG.

Will the converted file be smaller?

Often a PNG photo is larger than the JPEG source. File size depends on the image and the encoding.
