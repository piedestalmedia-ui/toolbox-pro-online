What the Percentage Calculator does

For “what percent of” calculations, the tool multiplies the base value by the percentage and divides by 100. Percentage change compares the difference with the absolute original value; when the original is zero, the change is undefined.

Useful for discounts, quick change comparisons, or understanding what fraction of a total a percentage represents.

How to use this tool

  1. Step 1. Enter the percentage and the base number for a part-of calculation.
  2. Step 2. Or enter the original and new values for percentage change.
  3. Step 3. Check the displayed result and confirm the inputs use the units you intend.

Limits and privacy

The calculator does not account for taxes, compound interest, or business-specific rounding rules. Results use ordinary decimal arithmetic.

Values are calculated in your browser and are not stored.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate 15% of 80?

Enter 15 as the percentage and 80 as the base. The result is 12.

How is percentage change calculated?

The tool compares the difference between the new and original values against the absolute value of the original. Starting from zero has no defined percentage change.
