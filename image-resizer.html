What the Image Resizer does

Image dimensions are measured in pixels. With proportions enabled, the browser scales the image to fit within both dimensions while retaining its original shape; the final dimensions may therefore be smaller than one of the values you entered.

Useful when a form, profile, or content brief requests an image no larger than a given pixel width and height.

How to use this tool

  1. Step 1. Choose an image from your device.
  2. Step 2. Enter the maximum output width and height in pixels.
  3. Step 3. Keep proportions on for a natural shape, then resize and download the copy.

Limits and privacy

Resizing can reduce visual detail when making an image smaller. If you turn off proportions, the image may be stretched or squashed. This tool does not upscale detail that was absent from the original.

The image is loaded and processed locally by your browser; it is not uploaded to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Why are the final dimensions not exactly what I entered?

With proportions enabled, the image fits inside the width and height you set. One dimension may be smaller to preserve its original shape.

Can I make a small image larger?

You can enter larger dimensions, but enlarging cannot restore detail and may make the image look soft.
