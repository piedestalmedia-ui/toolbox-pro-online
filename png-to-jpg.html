What the PNG to JPG does

JPEG is commonly used for photographs and can produce smaller files at a chosen quality, while PNG can preserve transparent pixels. JPEG has no transparency, so transparent areas are flattened during conversion.

Useful when a site or form accepts JPEG but not PNG, or when you need a standard photo format.

How to use this tool

  1. Step 1. Select a PNG image.
  2. Step 2. Choose Convert image.
  3. Step 3. Preview the JPG output and download the new copy.

Limits and privacy

JPEG does not support transparency and uses lossy compression. Review the converted image before replacing or sharing the original.

Frequently asked questions

What happens to transparent pixels?

JPEG cannot store transparency. Transparent regions are flattened by the browser during export; check the output against your intended background.

Is the original PNG changed?

No. Conversion creates a new downloadable JPEG copy.
